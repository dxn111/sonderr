THE START OF THE SLM GENERATION

Our first SLM. Built for Sonderr.

Sonderr-v1 is our first small language model: 0.6B parameters and specialized for Sonderr. We are building an SLM generation, not a general-purpose LLM: Sonderr will stay small by design. Its practical capability comes from deep specialization and the environment around it—web search, your workspace, and the right tools. Both will keep getting better.
